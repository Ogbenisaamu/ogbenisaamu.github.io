Project Overview

This project involved analyzing retail sales data and transforming it into an interactive Power BI dashboard. The analysis brings together sales, customer, and product information to provide a clearer view of overall business performance.

Business Objective

The objective was to create a dashboard that makes it easier to evaluate sales performance and identify differences in profitability across products, customer income levels, colors, and periods.

The dashboard also provides interactive filtering by region and product category, allowing users to explore the data from different perspectives.

Data Preparation & Analysis

The project involved preparing the underlying data before building the dashboard. Data was reviewed and transformed using Power Query, followed by analysis and dashboard development in Power BI.

The analysis focused on revenue, cost, profit, customer counts, product profitability, color-based sales performance, yearly revenue, monthly performance, and profit by customer income level.

Key Insights

  • Apple recorded the highest profitability among the brands shown in the dashboard.
  • White products represented the largest share of sales by color at 39.22%.
  • Medium-income customers generated the highest profit among the income-level groups displayed.
  • The dashboard shows monthly revenue variation across January, February, and March.
  • Revenue performance is presented for 2023, with the dashboard showing approximately ₦3M in total sales revenue.

Business Value

The dashboard converts raw sales information into an accessible visual reporting tool. It allows stakeholders to quickly monitor key performance indicators and explore profitability and sales patterns across different business dimensions.

This type of analysis can support discussions around product performance, customer segmentation, sales planning, and business performance monitoring.

Project Takeaway

This project strengthened my practical experience in data preparation, business intelligence, dashboard design, data visualization, and communicating analytical findings to a business audience.

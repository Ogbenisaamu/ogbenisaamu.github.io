Project Overview

This project brings together logistics data to examine shipment volume, billing trends, delivery timeliness, customer service, driver and truck performance, fuel expenditure, maintenance, downtime, and safety incidents.

The Four Dashboard Pages

Each page focuses on a different set of operational questions.

01. Executive Operations Overview

  • Total loads, billings, miles, and fuel costs
  • Monthly load volume and billing trends
  • On-time delivery trends
  • Facility activity and booking-type billings

02. Delivery & Customer Performance

  • On-time delivery by route
  • Route revenue compared with service performance
  • Facility detention performance
  • Customer delivery performance over time

03. Fleet & Driver Performance

  • Driver and truck counts
  • Driver workload and on-time performance
  • Truck utilization and fuel efficiency
  • Relationship between utilization and MPG

04. Cost, Fuel, Maintenance & Safety

  • Fuel expenditure and cost per mile
  • Maintenance cost and vehicle downtime
  • Fuel cost by route
  • Safety incidents and claims

Business Questions

  • How are shipment volume and billing changing over time?
  • Which routes and facilities show delivery-service challenges?
  • How are drivers and trucks performing?
  • How do truck utilization and fuel efficiency relate?
  • How do maintenance costs, downtime, and safety incidents affect operations?

Tools & Approach

The report uses Power BI visuals to bring operational measures and trends together across four analytical views. The project emphasizes KPI monitoring, comparisons over time, and relationships between service, fleet, cost, and safety measures.
